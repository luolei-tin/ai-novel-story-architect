# Chapter 04 Handoff — R1.0

State: CHAPTER_READY
Upstream: Chapter 3 PASS

## Objective
把“信任”和“合同边界”从理念变成实际成本。周衡必须决定：为了尽快拿到第一个租户，是不是应该给模糊承诺；林知微则逼他把承诺写成一个自己付得起的数字。

## Starting State
- liquid cash: approximately 10,000 RMB;
- Tenant B wants the shop but has not fully executed the lease;
- basic repair is estimated at 500–1,000 RMB;
- no rental collection yet;
- loading access is shared;
- Zhou has a 90-day cash ledger.

## Trigger
Tenant B agrees to sign if Zhou handles basic electrical and wall repairs before opening.
The tenant also asks for a verbal assurance that deliveries can be accommodated during busy days.

## Conflict
Zhou wants to say yes verbally to close the deal faster.
Lin points out that a verbal loading promise can become an unlimited future obligation.

## Choice
Zhou accepts a strict written support package:
- basic repair capped at 1,000 RMB;
- loading follows a written shared schedule;
- any extra request requires a new written agreement.

## Cost
The tenant is uncomfortable with the strict wording and almost withdraws.
Closing is delayed by two days.
Zhou may lose the tenant because he refuses convenient ambiguity.

## Fire
This is the first behavioral movement from 'I control outcomes' toward 'I define boundaries I can survive.'

## Constellation
Lin earns a small amount of credibility because she does not demand unlimited promises.
She is not emotionally close to Zhou and must not become instantly loyal.

## Foreshadowing
F05 should be planted strongly here:
future disputes will be constrained by the exact wording of this document.

## Ending State
- Tenant B lease = signed;
- repair commitment = 1,000 RMB cap;
- loading schedule = documented;
- no rent collected yet unless the prose explicitly records a signing deposit;
- liquid cash reduced only by any repair actually paid in the chapter;
- Lin's role = trusted enough to be consulted, not yet a decision-maker.

## Forbidden Moves
- no sudden landlord law or new contract rule;
- no narrator declaration that Zhou has become cautious;
- no full trust between Zhou and Lin;
- no unrecorded expense;
- no treating written limits as automatically accepted by every future tenant.

## Audit Targets
1. Is the tenant's near-withdrawal caused by the strict boundary?
2. Does the contract create future causal consequences?
3. Are all financial changes explicit?
4. Does Zhou choose a boundary despite a real short-term cost?
