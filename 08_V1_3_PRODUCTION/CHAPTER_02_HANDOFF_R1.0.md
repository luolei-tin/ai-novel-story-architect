# Chapter 02 Handoff — R1.0

Novel: 《重生后，我把一家旧商场做成了现金流》
State: CHAPTER_READY
Upstream: CHAPTER_01_CONDITIONAL_PASS

## Chapter Objective
让周衡在一个看似确定的未来机会前，第一次接受“记得方向，不等于知道时点”，并用现金流纪律约束自己的未来优势。

## Starting State
- Liquid cash: approximately 10,000 RMB.
- Operating right: committed, 180 days.
- Nearby wholesale market: expected to clear in approximately 90 days.
- Mall: 27 empty shops; three surviving merchants.
- Zhou does not yet have stable tenant cash flow.
- Lin Zhiyi is a skeptical local coordinator, not a trusted partner.

## New Trigger
A building-material supplier contacts Zhou with a prepayment deal: pay 4,000 RMB now and receive a substantial unit-price discount on renovation materials.
The supplier claims the price is temporary because a nearby construction wave is coming.

Zhou remembers that the district will indeed improve, but his memory does not contain a reliable exact start date.

## Character Intent
Zhou wants to preserve enough cash to survive the first 90 days.
He is tempted by the discount because it appears to reduce future costs.
His deeper fear is having too little liquidity when the first real operating problem arrives.

## Choice
Reject the 4,000 RMB prepayment.

## Cost
Pay a higher unit price later if materials are actually needed.
He also risks being wrong: the price may rise, meaning his caution has a measurable cost.

## Causal Chain
future memory suggests demand increase → supplier offers prepayment discount → Zhou cannot establish exact timing → 4,000 RMB would materially reduce remaining safety buffer → Zhou rejects the deal → later price movement becomes a live uncertainty.

## Information Control
Reader knows:
- Zhou remembers broad future trends.
- Zhou has only about 10,000 RMB remaining.
- supplier believes demand will increase.

Zhou knows:
- broad district improvement;
- his current cash position;
- no reliable exact date for the construction wave.

Supplier knows:
- current material pricing;
- its own sales expectations;
- not Zhou's future knowledge.

Unknown:
- exact date demand accelerates;
- exact future material price;
- exact amount of renovation required by the first tenants.

## Forbidden Moves
- Do not let Zhou know the exact future price.
- Do not make the supplier fraudulently fake the entire opportunity just to force the plot.
- Do not make Zhou reject the deal because the narrator declares the future.
- Do not make the 4,000 RMB immaterial; it must visibly affect his buffer.
- Do not give Zhou an unexplained new source of cash.
- Do not make Lin already understand Zhou's future knowledge.

## Foreshadowing
F01 from Chapter 1: verified collections as evidence.
F02 candidate: Zhou starts a handwritten 90-day cash ledger.
This ledger becomes later evidence of his operating discipline.

## Ending State
- Liquid cash remains approximately 10,000 RMB.
- Prepayment opportunity: rejected.
- Future-material price: unresolved.
- Zhou's operating discipline: strengthened.
- Immediate consequence: he now needs a real tenant before spending meaningful renovation money.
