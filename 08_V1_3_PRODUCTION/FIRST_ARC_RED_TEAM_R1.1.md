## Verdict

CONDITIONAL PASS for early production design.
The arc now contains sufficient causal preparation to authorize detailed Chapter 1 Handoff, but it is not a full-series lock.

## Evidence

### E01 — Mall necessity
Ch01 establishes: 30,000 RMB cash → 20,000 RMB performance deposit → 10,000 RMB liquidity.
The operating-right window and the 90-day merchant displacement window overlap.
Conclusion: the mall is now constrained by capital + time + infrastructure + tenant source, not merely future appreciation.

### E02 — Future-knowledge limitation
Ch02 explicitly makes the protagonist uncertain about exact timing and forces a present-day liquidity decision.
Conclusion: future knowledge is advantageous but not omniscient.

### E03 — Tenant choice prepares later conflict
Ch03 selects the lower-rent tenant because the higher-rent tenant demands renovation support, exclusivity and flexible loading.
Ch05 and Ch07 reuse the loading constraint.
Conclusion: the later operational conflict has a planted mechanical cause.

### E04 — Contract becomes causal
Ch04 creates hard caps on support and loading.
Ch05 creates a situation where violating those caps would create a short-term benefit.
Conclusion: the world rule is operational rather than decorative.

### E05 — Lin's non-substitutability
Ch08 requires Lin to mediate within a financial boundary and makes Zhou explicitly delegate authority.
Conclusion: her role changes the outcome rather than merely supplying exposition.

### E06 — Zhao's rationality
Ch10 frames Zhao's business model around control of future tenant flow and gives him a reason to cooperate before competing.
Conclusion: he is currently a rational commercial actor, not a plot villain.

### E07 — Opportunity cost
Ch11–Ch12 makes the new philosophy expensive: refusing leverage protects downside but leaves real revenue available to competitors.
Conclusion: the protagonist is choosing a value, not selecting an obviously superior option.

## Remaining Risks

### HIGH — Final Choice Still Not Closed
The first arc only establishes the direction of the theme.
The eventual control-versus-safety conflict remains OPEN.
Required: the middle arcs must produce at least two cases where increased founder control produces measurable fragility, while bounded shared authority produces a real short-term cost but improves resilience.

### MEDIUM — Legal/Operational Specificity
The exact operating agreement should be kept internally consistent. Do not introduce a new legal mechanism later merely to resolve a plot obstacle.

### MEDIUM — Commercial Numbers Must Remain State
Once Chapter 1–12 establish cash, deposits, promises, collections and costs, those numbers become continuity state.
Future chapters cannot silently reset the cash position.
