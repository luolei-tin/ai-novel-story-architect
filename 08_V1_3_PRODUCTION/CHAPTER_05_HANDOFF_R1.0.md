# Chapter 05 Handoff — R1.0

State: CHAPTER_READY
Upstream: Chapter 4 revised PASS

## Chapter Objective
把六号店从“签了合同”推进到“产生第一批真实经营数据”，并让周衡面对第一笔应该花还是不应该花的钱。

## Starting State
- liquid cash ≈ 9,620 RMB;
- Tenant B lease signed;
- rent collected = 0 RMB;
- repair spending = 380 RMB;
- remaining repair commitment cap = up to 1,000 RMB;
- loading schedule documented;
- no category exclusivity;
- Zhou maintains a 90-day cash ledger;
- Lin remains an independent operating-information source.

## Trigger
Tenant B is preparing to open but customer visibility is weak.
A low-cost traffic test is possible for 1,200 RMB.
Full facade renovation would cost several thousand RMB and would consume too much liquidity.

## Choice
Zhou chooses the 1,200 RMB measurable traffic experiment rather than cosmetic renovation.

## Cost
Cash falls from approximately 9,620 RMB to approximately 8,420 RMB.
The first week performs below expectation, so the spending does not produce an immediate reward.

## Character
Zhou must accept a controlled, measurable loss in exchange for information.
He should not use future knowledge to declare the result in advance.

## Required State Change
- first real customer-flow data exists;
- first operating expense beyond maintenance is incurred;
- actual collection may begin only if explicitly caused by tenant sales and contract terms;
- Zhou learns something concrete about traffic rather than receiving a miracle success.

## Foreshadow
The single loading window should create a small operational inconvenience during the traffic event, preparing the larger Chapter 7 conflict.

## Forbidden
- no sudden viral promotion;
- no guaranteed traffic because Zhou remembers the future;
- no unexplained rent collection;
- no new financing;
- no full mall transformation.

## Audit Targets
1. Is the 1,200 RMB spend a real choice?
2. Is the weak first-week result causally credible?
3. Does the experiment generate evidence that can influence later decisions?
4. Is the 8,420 RMB cash state preserved if no other explicit transaction occurs?
