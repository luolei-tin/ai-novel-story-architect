## Important Status Correction

The earlier Chapter 1–4 prose experiment passed the original logic and continuity checks, but a later Dramatic Integrity audit identified a more fundamental quality failure:

> the prose operates as an expanded outline rather than sustained drama.

Therefore the earlier chapter-level PASS results remain historical audit records, but they do not establish acceptable literary quality.

DIAGNOSTIC / NON-CANONICAL QUALITY BASELINE

They must not be treated as the novel's definitive prose standard.

## Root Cause

The previous production loop over-weighted:
- event causality;
- accounting continuity;
- world-rule consistency;
and under-weighted:
- competing scene objectives;
- character tactics;
- reversals;
- subtext;
- meaningful opposition;
- independent NPC agency;
- choice-generated consequences.

## New Hard Gate

A chapter must pass both:
1. Logical Integrity
2. Dramatic Integrity

Logical PASS without Dramatic PASS is not sufficient.

- relationship engine;
- first-act reversals;
- chapter-level dramatic situations.

Only after redesign should new prose be generated.
