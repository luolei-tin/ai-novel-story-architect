# Chapter 03 Handoff — R1.0

State: CHAPTER_READY
Upstream: Chapter 2 PASS

## Chapter Objective
让周衡第一次面对真正的商业诱惑：一个高租金、高预付款的租户，看起来能迅速改善现金流，但附带的装修、排他和装卸条件会把未来风险埋进合同。

## Starting State
- liquid cash: approximately 10,000 RMB;
- no rental income yet;
- 180-day operating right active;
- 27 empty shops;
- three existing merchants;
- nearby market has roughly 90 days before clearance;
- Zhou's 4,000 RMB material prepayment was rejected;
- Lin remains skeptical and independent.

## Tenant A
Offer:
- monthly rent = 1,800 RMB;
- three months prepaid = 5,400 RMB;
- asks for 8,000 RMB renovation support;
- wants category exclusivity;
- wants flexible loading hours.

## Tenant B
Offer:
- monthly rent = 1,300 RMB;
- no prepayment;
- asks for only basic repair within a strict 1,000 RMB cap;
- accepts shared loading schedule;
- fits the surrounding merchant mix.

## Critical Choice
Zhou chooses Tenant B.

The choice must hurt.
He is consciously giving up 5,400 RMB of immediate prepayment and a higher nominal monthly rent.

## Why He Refuses A
- the 8,000 RMB support exceeds his cash buffer;
- loading flexibility conflicts with an existing merchant's use;
- category exclusivity would reduce tenant-mix flexibility;
- the prepayment is attractive but does not remove those liabilities.

## Character Requirement
Zhou initially wants A because the immediate cash feels like safety.
He must notice the contradiction himself: more cash today can create less room to maneuver tomorrow.

## Lin's Role
Lin does not tell Zhou what to choose.
She provides current merchant information:
A's category would conflict with an existing business and its loading demand would likely become contentious.

She does not reveal anything about Zhou's future knowledge.

## Foreshadowing
F03: loading access is a scarce shared resource.
F04: category mix affects customer flow and merchant survival.
F05: written limits will later matter because verbal flexibility becomes expensive.

## Ending State
- Tenant B is conditionally accepted;
- no lease should yet be treated as fully executed until the next chapter's contract discussion;
- Zhou has deliberately sacrificed a near-term cash inflow;
- relationship with Lin changes from pure information utility to tentative professional respect;
- loading conflict is now a planted concrete mechanism.

## Audit Targets
1. Is A genuinely tempting?
2. Is refusing A measurably costly?
3. Does Lin add information that Zhou cannot possess from future memory?
4. Is the loading conflict planted concretely?
5. Does the chapter avoid giving Zhou perfect certainty?
