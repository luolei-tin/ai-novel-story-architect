# First Arc Outline Candidate — R1.0

Novel: 《重生后，我把一家旧商场做成了现金流》
State: OUTLINE_DRAFT
Range: Chapters 1–12

## Arc Objective

Turn Zhou Heng from a man with future knowledge into a small operator who has to convert that knowledge into present-day, auditable cash flow.

The first arc must prove four things:

1. the mall opportunity is real but fragile;
2. Zhou's advantage cannot replace local information and trust;
3. his control instinct creates at least one measurable business problem;
4. the first stable cash-flow result comes from a choice and a relationship, not a lucky prophecy.

## Chapter Grid

### Ch01 — 拍卖名单上的旧商场
Quest:
Zhou discovers the 180-day operating-right window and confirms he has exactly enough cash for the performance deposit.

Choice:
He rejects a tempting but speculative use of the 30,000 RMB and commits almost all liquid capital to the operating right.

Fire:
His old instinct says "own the asset"; the new constraint forces him to accept "operate first."

Constellation:
He notices Lin Zhiyi during a merchant dispute but initially treats her as a source of local information.

End State:
Deposit committed; personal liquidity reduced from 30,000 to 10,000 RMB.

### Ch02 — 十万块的未来，不值一万块现金
Quest:
Zhou maps which memories are reliable and which are not, then builds a survival budget.

Choice:
He deliberately ignores a remembered high-return opportunity because it would consume the working capital needed for the mall.

Fire:
First behavioral proof that survival outranks speculative profit.

Constellation:
Lin refuses to work under an operator who promises more than he can fund.

End State:
Written operating budget; Lin agrees only to a trial role.

### Ch03 — 第一间铺子
Quest:
Find the first tenant.

Choice:
Zhou chooses a modest tenant with complementary demand rather than the highest offered rent.

Fire:
He begins to separate "price" from "quality of cash flow."

Constellation:
Lin challenges his choice and then provides a merchant introduction.

End State:
First lease signed; first tenant becomes the anchor candidate.

### Ch04 — 不收满租金
Quest:
Prepare the first cluster of three shops.

Choice:
Zhou accepts a lower initial rent with explicit, bounded support instead of a high-rent tenant who demands uncertain concessions.

Fire:
His old control instinct conflicts with a new operating principle.

Constellation:
Lin's credibility is put at risk because she recommended the tenant.

End State:
Three tenants are conditionally committed; support promises are documented.

### Ch05 — 开门第一周
Quest:
Generate actual collections.

Choice:
Zhou spends part of the remaining cash on a measurable traffic experiment rather than cosmetic renovation.

Fire:
He accepts a controlled loss as the price of obtaining real evidence.

Constellation:
Lin gains leverage because her merchant feedback changes the traffic plan.

End State:
First real operating data; cash remains positive but thinner than expected.

### Ch06 — 账上有钱，手里没钱
Quest:
Handle a collection mismatch.

Choice:
Zhou delays one optional improvement instead of using informal borrowing.

Fire:
He discovers liquidity and accounting profit are different kinds of safety.

Constellation:
Lin sees that Zhou will actually honor a spending boundary.

End State:
A short-term cash squeeze is absorbed without emergency debt.

### Ch07 — 电箱问题
Quest:
Resolve an anchor tenant's electrical and loading demand.

Choice:
Zhou tries to impose a rational compromise himself.

Fire:
His belief that better analysis equals better control is exposed as incomplete.

Constellation:
The two tenant relationships deteriorate because Zhou does not understand which promise is socially non-negotiable.

End State:
Operations partially blocked; Zhou's direct approach fails.

### Ch08 — 林知微不肯替他兜底
Quest:
Repair the tenant conflict.

Choice:
Zhou asks Lin to solve it, but must accept her condition: no unfunded promise.

Fire:
He relinquishes a small piece of decision authority.

Constellation:
Lin becomes a real partner rather than a subordinate helper.

End State:
Written settlement; loading access restored; electrical plan deferred.

### Ch09 — 第一个稳定月
Quest:
Prove recurring collections.

Choice:
Zhou turns the early data into a repeatable tenant-selection rule rather than chasing another hot opportunity.

Fire:
He starts treating systems as protection instead of personal control.

Constellation:
Trust becomes bounded and evidence-based.

End State:
First positive recurring cash-flow month.

### Ch10 — 赵启盛第一次递名片
Quest:
Respond to a mature operator's interest.

Choice:
Zhou refuses an unnecessarily aggressive confrontation and preserves a channel for cooperation.

Fire:
He learns that control does not require eliminating every competitor.

Constellation:
Zhao begins to treat Zhou as a rational operator; rivalry becomes economically grounded.

End State:
Zhao identifies the mall as a meaningful future asset.

### Ch11 — 九十天倒计时
Quest:
The nearby wholesale market demolition accelerates; more merchants seek relocation.

Choice:
Zhou refuses to overfill the mall because the new tenants would require cash commitments he cannot safely fund.

Fire:
He chooses survivability over speed despite knowing the district will appreciate.

Constellation:
Lin supports the restraint, creating the first explicit shared operating principle.

End State:
Opportunity expands, but Zhou deliberately leaves money on the table.

### Ch12 — 不是所有的钱都该拿
Quest:
A larger tenant package appears that could rapidly increase revenue but requires debt and broad guarantees.

Choice:
Zhou rejects it.

Fire:
This is the first major proof that his new identity is built around downside containment.

Constellation:
Lin's trust becomes contingent on the fact that Zhou can say no to his own temptation.

End State:
The mall is stable but under-expanded.
New threat:
Zhao now has the capital capacity to pursue the same tenant package indirectly.

## Arc Turning Points

TP01:
Zhou chooses the mall over a speculative future-money opportunity.

TP02:
Zhou chooses stable tenant economics over maximum rent.

TP03:
Zhou's direct-control strategy fails at the electrical/loading dispute.

TP04:
Zhou accepts Lin's bounded intervention.

TP05:
Zhou rejects debt-funded expansion despite clear upside.

## Required Foreshadowing Candidates

F01:
A small contract clause concerning collection verification appears in Ch02 and becomes important when financing is considered later.

F02:
Lin warns that one broken merchant promise can travel through the entire local merchant network.

F03:
Zhao mentions that he cares about "控制权的连续性" rather than immediate rent, planting the later governance conflict.

## Arc Exit Condition

The arc may proceed to Chapter 13 only if:

- the first stable cash-flow month is supported by actual chapter events;
- no future knowledge has been used as unexplained omniscience;
- Zhou's Chapter 7 failure is causally prepared;
- Lin's Chapter 8 intervention is earned by prior relationship setup;
- Zhao's later threat is based on independent capital economics;
- the five turning points survive delete-node testing.
