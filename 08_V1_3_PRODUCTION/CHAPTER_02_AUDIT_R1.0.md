## Causality

E01:
Supplier offers 4,000 RMB prepayment discount.
Zhou's cash is approximately 10,000 RMB.
No stable tenant revenue exists.
Therefore the 4,000 RMB reduction in liquidity is materially risky.

E02:
Zhou's memory gives broad district improvement, but no reliable exact timing.
Therefore he cannot rationally treat the discount as certain future savings.

E03:
He rejects prepayment and accepts the possibility of paying more later.
The chapter therefore contains a genuine opportunity cost.

## Character

The choice matches the established wound:
Zhou associates excessive leverage and loss of fallback options with his previous collapse.

Important:
He does not reject the deal because it is "bad."
He rejects it because the timing uncertainty makes the liquidity cost unacceptable.

This preserves the character contradiction without resolving it too quickly.

## Information Integrity

No exact future construction date or material price is disclosed.
The protagonist's future knowledge remains probabilistic and incomplete.

No other character is given knowledge of Zhou's private future memory.
