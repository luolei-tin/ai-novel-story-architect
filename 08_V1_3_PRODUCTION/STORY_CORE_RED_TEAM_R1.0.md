# Story Core Red-Team Audit — R1.0

## Artifact

- Novel: 《重生后，我把一家旧商场做成了现金流》
- Artifact: STORY_CORE_DRAFT_R1.0
- State: CORE_DRAFT
- Verdict: REVISE BEFORE CHARACTER_LOCKED

## Finding 1 — HIGH: The Old Mall Is Not Yet Causally Necessary

### Observed

The seed states that the old mall is about to be auctioned and contains 27 empty shops plus three mistrusting merchants.

### Broken Rule

Story Core requires necessity:

> If the protagonist refuses the central premise, the mainline should not still naturally function.

### Why It Fails

At present, the mall is an attractive stage but not yet a necessary causal engine.

The protagonist's stated philosophy — building durable cash flow — could theoretically be tested in another business.

### Minimum Repair

Before locking the core, establish a specific chain:

`refusing the mall → loss of a unique opportunity/resource/relationship → inability to test the protagonist's new operating philosophy → different character trajectory`

The mall must create a constraint that another business cannot trivially replace.

---

## Finding 2 — HIGH: Final Choice Is Still Interchangeable

### Observed

Two candidate endings remain plausible:

- accept a capital deal and give up control;
- reject it and retain control while carrying greater risk.

### Broken Rule

The Final Choice must emerge from character truth and become structurally necessary.

### Why It Fails

Both choices can currently be made to support the thesis through sufficiently convenient consequences.

That means the thesis has not yet constrained the ending.

### Minimum Repair

The middle and late structure must create a situation where:

- control and safety become mutually exclusive;
- the partner/team cannot remain protected under total founder control;
- accepting the deal requires a psychologically meaningful relinquishment;
- rejecting the deal must recreate a recognizable version of Zhou Heng's old behavior, not merely create abstract financial risk.

Only then can the ending answer the Central Question rather than merely select a market outcome.

---

## Finding 3 — MEDIUM: "Future Knowledge" Has Not Yet Acquired a Cost

### Observed

The protagonist knows the future but cannot control it.

### Risk

Without an explicit information constraint, future knowledge can become a universal problem-solving device.

### Minimum Repair

Define a knowledge degradation rule:

- what future facts are remembered clearly;
- what is remembered only approximately;
- what becomes unreliable after the protagonist changes events;
- what information he could never know because he was not previously involved.

This should affect decisions, not merely lore.

---

## Finding 4 — MEDIUM: Commercial Opposition Needs Independent Objective

The future antagonist cannot exist only to oppose the protagonist.

Minimum requirement:

- independent asset/incentive;
- rational reason to want the mall;
- rational reason not to simply copy the protagonist;
- a point where cooperation is preferable to conflict;
- a point where conflict becomes unavoidable.

---

## Current Verdict

`REVISE`

The premise is strong enough to continue as a Core Draft.

It is **not** strong enough to declare `CORE_LOCKED`.

Do not start chapter prose yet.

## Next Required Work

1. close the mall-necessity chain;
2. define the non-substitutable relationship;
3. define future-knowledge limits;
4. define antagonist economics;
5. rebuild the Final Choice after those constraints exist.
