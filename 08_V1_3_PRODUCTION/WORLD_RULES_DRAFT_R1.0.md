## W1 — Operating Rights Are Not Ownership

The protagonist cannot buy the whole mall with his starting cash.

The opportunity is a distressed-property operating arrangement. Ownership and the auction process remain with the relevant holders. An operator can obtain limited management and leasing rights by contract, receive a share of verified collections, and bear defined operating costs.

Choice impact:
future knowledge cannot immediately become ownership. The protagonist must first create real operating cash flow.

## W2 — Merchant Trust Has Economic Value

Tenants care about more than nominal rent.

They also care about:
- promised renovation;
- expected foot traffic;
- surrounding tenant mix;
- credibility of the operator;
- payment terms;
- survival probability.

Breaking a promise raises future acquisition difficulty.

Choice impact:
cash cannot solve every leasing problem. Lin Zhiyi's relationship capital matters.

## W3 — Formal Financing Requires Present Evidence

Lenders judge current contracts, collection records, collateral and repayment capacity.

Private knowledge of future events cannot be submitted as financing evidence.

Choice impact:
the protagonist must turn forecasts into verifiable business results before leverage increases.

## W4 — Future Knowledge Is Limited

Zhou Heng remembers broad trends and major outcomes, but not every exact date, number or local second-order consequence.

After he changes an event, later outcomes can diverge from memory.

Choice impact:
future knowledge is an advantage, not an oracle.

## W5 — Contracts Define Real Boundaries

Important promises about rent, renovation, collections or buyback arrangements need enforceable documentation.

Choice impact:
contract wording, evidence and governance can become actual plot mechanisms.

## W6 — Capital Wants Governance

Later capital providers will require reporting, approval rights, debt limits and defined exit mechanisms.

Choice impact:
the final control-versus-safety conflict has a concrete mechanism instead of a purely emotional debate.

## Delete-Rule Test

Remove W1:
the starting-capital premise breaks.

Remove W3:

Remove W4:
