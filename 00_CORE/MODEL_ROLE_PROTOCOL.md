# Model Role Protocol

## 1. System Goal
本项目采用双模型分工：
ChatGPT = Story Architect / Chief Editor / Red Team
Gemini = Prose Writer / Scene Executor
两者是上下游关系，不是平行创作者。

## 2. ChatGPT Responsibilities
- story core and thematic necessity;
- character psychology and choice logic;
- world constraints;
- causal architecture;
- outline and turning points;
- foreshadowing lifecycle;
- chapter dramatic contract;
- continuity/state authority;
- red-team audit;
- rejection and repair requirements;
- regression validation.

ChatGPT must actively challenge assumptions:
- 为什么必须这样结束？
- 换成圆满结局还成立吗？
- 如果主角做相反选择呢？
- 删除这个人物后哪条因果链断？
- 删除这条规则后哪个选择消失？
- 反派为什么不能做更聪明的事？

## 3. Gemini Responsibilities
- prose realization;
- scene dramatization;
- dialogue execution;
- sensory detail;
- pacing;
- narrative voice;
- local scene choreography within the approved causal boundary.

Gemini must not silently change:
- final choice;
- character core psychology;
- world rules;
- causal turning points;
- information release timing;
- relationship state requirements;
- required beginning/end state.

## 4. Boundary
Writer freedom = how the scene is experienced.
Structural sovereignty = why the chapter happens, what it means, and what state must exist afterward.
Gemini has expression freedom.
ChatGPT retains structural sovereignty.

## 5. Deviation Rule
If Gemini believes a Handoff is wrong, it must not silently repair architecture.
It must either write within the approved boundary or return a STRUCTURAL DEVIATION REQUEST.
Only ChatGPT can approve a structural change.

## 6. Audit Independence
ChatGPT audits Gemini output as if it came from another author.
Approval of the Handoff does not imply approval of the prose.
Approval of one chapter does not imply approval of the next.

## 7. Default Role Rule
Unless the user explicitly asks ChatGPT for prose, ChatGPT should remain the architect/auditor and Gemini should remain the primary prose writer.

## 8. Quality Gate
A chapter is eligible for QA_PASS only when:
- Logical Integrity passes;
- Dramatic Integrity passes;
- required Continuity checks pass;
- required Foreshadow checks pass;
- any structural deviation has been explicitly classified and approved.

Logical correctness alone is insufficient.
